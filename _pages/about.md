<div class="academic-intro">
  <p class="lead-copy">
    I am a <strong>Computer Science researcher</strong> working on string algorithms, data compression, pattern matching, and text indexing, with additional research interests in quantum algorithms.
  </p>

  <p>
    I hold a <strong>Ph.D. in Computer Science</strong> from the
    <a href="https://www.univ-rouen.fr" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Rouen Normandy</a>
    and am completing the University of Catania side of a joint Ph.D. (<em>cotutelle</em>) with the
    <a href="https://www.unict.it" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Catania</a>,
    under the supervision of
    <a href="https://www.dmi.unict.it/faro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Simone Faro</a>
    and
    <a href="http://www-igm.univ-mlv.fr/~lecroq/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Thierry Lecroq</a>.
  </p>

  <p>
    My research has included visiting periods at the
    <a href="https://www.yamanashi.ac.jp/english/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">University of Yamanashi</a>
    and
    <a href="https://www.unicamp.br/unicamp/english" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">UNICAMP</a>,
    with collaborations spanning Italy, France, Japan and Brazil.
  </p>
